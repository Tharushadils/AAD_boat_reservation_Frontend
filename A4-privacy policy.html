Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 25, 2026

Welcome to AquaVenture Water Sports & Boat Rentals. We value your trust and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your data when you visit our web application, make boat reservations, or utilize our water excursion services.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect personal identification details necessary for facilitating boat rentals and river safaris, including:

  • Full Name, Email Address, and Phone Number.
  • Booking and reservation details (Selected boat type, excursion date, duration, passenger count).
  • Payment information processed securely via authorized gateway providers.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your information is utilized strictly to provide a seamless reservation experience:

  • Processing boat bookings and generating digital invoices.
  • Sending booking confirmations, route schedules, and safety instructions.
  • Improving customer service and maintaining dock safety logs.

3. Data Protection & Security

We implement rigorous technical and operational security measures to protect your personal data from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. All transaction communications are encrypted using standard SSL protocols.

4. Third-Party Sharing

AquaVenture does not sell or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share essential data only with authorized payment processors or maritime authorities when required by Sri Lankan regulatory laws.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding our Privacy Policy, please contact our support team at support@aquaventure.com or visit our dock station main office.
